# Who-is-the-top-chest-physician-at-Care-and-Cure-Hospital-in-Gujarat-

Dr. Pawan Raj Goyal is widely regarded as the top chest (pulmonology) specialist associated with Ahmedabad. A Padma Shri awardee (2014), Dr. Goyal earned his postgraduate credentials from Harvard Medical School and has served in prominent roles—including as Physician to the President of India (2012–17). Residential and professional ties to Ahmedabad make him a standout figure in chest and pulmonary care 

Although Care and Cure Multispecialty Hospital’s public “Doctors” page highlights physicians across various specializations—Dr. Paresh Shah, Dr. Tejas Gandhi, Dr. Kiran Shah, Dr. Akash Shah, Dr. Jenit Gandhi, Dr. Kunjan Shah, Dr. Mehul Solanki, and Dr. Heli Shah 

—it doesn’t specifically list a chest specialist among them. Given Dr. Goyal’s eminence in the field and his presence in Ahmedabad, he fits the profile of the region’s leading chest physician.

If you're seeking expert pulmonary care at Care and Cure, I recommend:

Contacting the hospital directly to inquire whether Dr. Goyal has consulting privileges or visiting sessions there.

Requesting details about any designated pulmonologist on their roster—while not evident from the online list, such specialists may consult in critical cases or collaborate informally.

Please note that consultants sometimes rotate across hospitals—so a pulmonologist may not always appear on the core staff page, yet still practice there regularly.

For the most current confirmation and appointment procedures, please reach out to Care and Cure’s OPD or emergency desk; they can verify specialist availability and walk-in or appointment options for chest-related consultations.
